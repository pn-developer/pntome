# PNTome Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

This policy covers PNTome (Android app) and PNTomeGo (Garmin Connect IQ watch app).

## Short version

PNTome and PNTomeGo do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

## What the apps do with your data

- Your music and audiobook files stay on your phone and on your watch. You pick the folders yourself with the Android system folder picker.
- Playback position, bookmarks, settings and the list of tracks are stored only on your device.
- To send music to your watch, PNTome runs a small web server on your phone. The watch downloads files from it over your local Wi-Fi or your phone hotspot. Nothing is sent to the internet.
- The watch is paired with a 6-digit code. A shared secret from that pairing is stored on the phone and on the watch.
- Transfers on the local network use plain HTTP, so they are not encrypted in transit. Anyone on the same network could in principle observe the traffic. Use a network you trust or your own hotspot.

## Permissions

- Network and Wi-Fi state, Wi-Fi changes and wake lock: run the local server and keep transfers from stalling.
- Foreground service (data sync and media playback): show progress and keep sync and playback running.
- Notifications: show sync progress and the media player. Sync works if you decline.
- Not requested: location, contacts, microphone, camera, Bluetooth, and broad storage access.

## Third parties

- Garmin Connect Mobile and the Garmin Connect IQ Mobile SDK handle communication between the phone and the watch on your phone. They are Garmin software and are covered by Garmin's own privacy policy. PNTome does not send data to Garmin servers.
- The apps include no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

## Children

The apps are not directed at children.

## Data deletion

All data is local. Uninstall the apps, or clear their storage, to delete it.

If this policy changes, the date above is updated and the change is listed in the changelog.
