## Short description

Listen to audiobooks and music from your phone on your watch, offline, with bookmarks and resume.

## Long description

PNTomeGo is the watch companion of PNTome, an Android app for audiobooks and music.

Pick folders in PNTome on your phone. PNTomeGo copies the audio to your watch over Wi-Fi, so you can leave the phone at home and still listen.

Features
- Sync audiobooks and music from your phone to the watch over Wi-Fi
- Play from the watch's music player with Bluetooth headphones
- Bookmarks and resume where you stopped
- No account, no ads, no analytics

Requirements
- PNTome installed on your Android phone (required)
- Garmin Connect Mobile installed and paired with your watch (required)
- A supported Garmin watch with music storage and Wi-Fi
- A Wi-Fi network shared by the watch and the phone while syncing

Supported watches
- fenix 6S Pro, fenix 6 Pro (including Solar), fenix 6X Pro
- fenix 8 AMOLED 43 mm and 47 mm
- fenix 8 Pro 47 mm
- fenix 8 Solar 47 mm and 51 mm

PNTomeGo does not work on its own. It only plays content that PNTome has synced to it.

## What's new (first release)

First public release of PNTomeGo (formerly Gsound watch app). Sync audiobooks and music from PNTome over Wi-Fi, play on the watch, and resume from bookmarks. Reinstall PNTome on the phone together with this version if you used an earlier test build.
